Water bake-off — OCEAN

Real cycler: 3 frames (anim-2 → anim-3 → anim-4), 450 ms each, one global phase for every tile, loop wraps anim-4 → anim-2. Coastline fringes are static by design — they never animate in the shipped renderer.

Each panel: 12×7 tiles of open water with a beach coastline along the top (real beach sheet + the candidate's own edge-n / corner fringe cells). "Stream-in flash" repaints base-0..3 for one tick, which is exactly what a newly streamed chunk shows before the next global water tick catches it.
